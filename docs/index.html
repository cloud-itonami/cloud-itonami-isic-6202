ISIC 6202 · Governed SaaS Blueprint

The problem: AI-powered support automation can normalize cases and draft status changes—but without guardrails, it commits faster response-times than an account's plan entitles, skips valid case-lifecycle steps, or auto-publishes embargoed knowledge without review.

The answer: An independent ServiceGovernor holds every unsafe commit. It audits SLA entitlement, case-sequence validity, and refund risk before the LLM draft ever persists. All decisions become immutable, auditable records.

What this is: OSS governance layer for a customer-service-hub SaaS platform (the HubSpot Service Hub / Salesforce Service Cloud class). Fork, deploy, run, improve, and sell—published under AGPL-3.0.

What this is not: Sales-pipeline governance, IT-ticket routing, marketing automation, or AI-magic (it is plain actor-model state machines and ledger).
